# Prism Paddock

<img src="./images/cover.png" alt="Prism Paddock" width="320" />

A gentle little unicorn is waiting for you in a meadow. Her rainbow has faded to
grey... and since that rainbow is her only source of food, she is in real
trouble!  
Roll up your sleeves, feed her, tidy up after her, and bring every
color back to the sky, one shade (and enigma) at a time.

## Your Goal

Restore the seven colors of the rainbow.

Unlock them by making the paddock more beautiful. When the last color returns, the rainbow is whole again and the unicorn is happy for good.

## How to Play

The game revolves around a simple, cozy loop:

1. **Feed the unicorn.** Open the **🌈 Feed** tab to reveal the palette of
   rainbow fragments: drag any unlocked fragment onto the unicorn to feed her.
2. **Let her digest.** Well fed and happy, she takes a little stroll across the
   meadow and eventually leaves a **butterfly-poop** behind.
3. **Clean up.** Tap a butterfly-poop to clean it. Each one you clear rewards
   you with **butterflies** of that color.
4. **Decorate.** Spend your butterflies to grow decorations around the paddock.
   With new deocrations, you can unlock new colors!

## Butterflies

Butterflies are your currency. You collect them by cleaning up after the
unicorn, and you spend them to grow decorations. Is it gross? No! It's magical!

## Talking to the Unicorn

The unicorn is your companion and your guide. She greets you, cheers you on, and
gently nudges you toward what to try next. **Tap her** any time to hear her
repeat her last words.

## Tips

- Feeding leads to poop, and poop leads to butterflies: so keep her well fed.
- Don't hoard butterflies: spending them is the way to make some progress!
- Stuck on a color? Re-read the unicorn's hints; the clues are all
  there.
- There's no way to lose and no timer. Oh, and there is also an auto-save!

## After the Rainbow

Once every color is back, the game is **won**. But you're welcome to stay :)  
Keep feeding, keep decorating, and turn the paddock into whatever colorful little
world you like.

Happy gardening, and thank you for saving the little unicorn!
